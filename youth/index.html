Youth Club Wrestling

Grades K-12. Build confidence, learn fundamentals, and have fun on the mat. No experience necessary.

About the Program

At 223 Wrestling, our purpose is to support and grow the sport of wrestling within the Fowlerville community and the surrounding local area. We foster a culture of resilience and disciplined learning, where the focus remains on the individual progress of each athlete. We welcome all wrestlers who share our commitment to hard work and character, providing a supportive, positive environment to reach their highest potential on the mat and in life.

What to Expect

1

Warm-Up & Drills

Each practice starts with stretching, jogging, and wrestling-specific drills to build coordination.

2

Technique Instruction

Coaches demonstrate and break down moves step-by-step, then wrestlers practice with partners.

3

Live Wrestling

Practice ends with situational and live wrestling so kids can apply what they learned.
